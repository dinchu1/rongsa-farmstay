Welcome to Rongsa

Where Mountains Meet Tradition

Nestled in the serene village of Sittong 2, Lower Mamring Busty, Rongsa Farmstay offers an authentic escape into the heart of Darjeeling's Lepcha heritage.

Your Home in the Hills

Rongsa Farmstay is more than just accommodation—it's an invitation to experience the authentic rhythm of village life in the Darjeeling hills. As members of the indigenous Lepcha community, we welcome you to share in our traditions, stories, and the natural beauty that surrounds us.

Wake up to the sound of the river flowing nearby, take a refreshing dip in our pool overlooking the mountains, and immerse yourself in the tranquility that only Sittong 2 can offer.

Explore Sittong 2

Nearby Attractions & Experiences

Sittong 2 village in Lower Mamring Busty offers the perfect blend of natural beauty and cultural richness, away from crowded tourist spots.

🏔️ Mountain Viewpoints

Stunning views of the Eastern Himalayas and Kanchenjunga range from nearby hilltops.

🍃 Tea Gardens

Visit authentic Darjeeling tea estates and learn about tea cultivation and processing.

💧 Waterfalls & Rivers

Explore hidden waterfalls and pristine river streams perfect for nature walks.

🙏 Monasteries & Culture

Visit nearby Buddhist monasteries and experience local Lepcha and Tibetan traditions.

🥾 Trekking Trails

Multiple trekking routes through dense forests and scenic village paths.

🌄 Village Life

Experience authentic village life, interact with locals, and taste traditional cuisine.
