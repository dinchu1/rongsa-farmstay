Location & Directions

Getting Here

How to Reach Rongsa Farmstay

By Air

Nearest Airport: Bagdogra (IXB) - 55 km

  • Fly to Bagdogra Airport
  • Hire taxi/cab to Sittong (2 hours)
  • We can arrange pickup for groups

By Train

Nearest Station: New Jalpaiguri (NJP) - 50 km

  • Arrive at NJP Railway Station
  • Take shared jeep/taxi to Kurseong
  • Local taxi from Kurseong to Sittong

By Road

From Siliguri: 45 km via Kurseong

  • Take NH110 towards Kurseong
  • From Kurseong, follow signs to Sittong
  • Roads are well-maintained but hilly
  • GPS coordinates: 26.9124°N, 88.2636°E

💡 Pro Tip: For first-time visitors, we recommend hiring a local taxi from Kurseong who knows the route well. Contact us for trusted driver recommendations!

🌐 GPS Users: Search for "Sittong, Lower Mamring" on Google Maps and follow directions. Call us if you need navigation help.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is advance booking required?

Yes, we recommend booking at least 3-5 days in advance, especially for weekends and holidays. Contact us to check availability.

Do you provide transportation?

We can arrange pickup from Kurseong or Siliguri for an additional charge. Please request this when booking.

What's included in the room rate?

All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), pool access, guided village walks, and basic amenities are included at ₹1,500 per person per night.

Is mobile network available?

Yes, major networks (Airtel, Jio, Vodafone) have coverage. We also provide WiFi in common areas.

What's the best time to visit?

March-May and October-December are ideal. Monsoon (June-September) offers lush greenery but occasional heavy rain.

Are children and elderly welcome?

Absolutely! Families are welcome. The property is safe for children, though some areas are on slopes.
