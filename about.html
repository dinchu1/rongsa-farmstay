Our Story

Welcome to Our Home

The Rongsa Family Story

Rongsa Farmstay was born from a simple dream: to share the beauty of our ancestral home and the richness of Lepcha culture with travelers seeking authentic experiences beyond typical tourist destinations.

Our family belongs to the Lepcha community, the indigenous people of Sikkim and the Darjeeling hills. For generations, our ancestors have lived in harmony with these mountains, cultivating the land, preserving traditions, and maintaining a deep spiritual connection with nature.

Located in Sittong 2, Lower Mamring Busty, our farmstay sits on land that has been in our family for decades. What began as a simple family home has evolved into a welcoming space where guests can experience genuine village life while enjoying modern comforts.

Indigenous Roots

The Lepcha Heritage

The Lepcha people are the original inhabitants of the Eastern Himalayas, known for their deep connection to nature and rich cultural traditions.

🏔️ Children of the Mountains

The word "Lepcha" means "children of the snow" or "children of the peaks." Our ancestors have lived in these Himalayan foothills for centuries, developing a profound understanding of the land.

🌱 Nature's Guardians

Lepcha culture emphasizes living in harmony with nature. Traditional practices include sustainable farming, respect for sacred forests, and worship of mountain deities.

🎭 Rich Traditions

From traditional music and dance to unique cuisine and handicrafts, Lepcha culture is vibrant and alive. We love sharing our traditions with guests.

🙏 Spiritual Connection

Lepcha spirituality blends Buddhism with indigenous animistic beliefs. Sacred mountains, rivers, and forests are central to our worldview.

Life in Sittong

Village Living at Its Finest

Sittong village, where Rongsa Farmstay calls home, is a hidden gem in the Darjeeling hills. Unlike crowded tourist towns, Sittong retains its authentic village character.

Life here moves at a different pace. Mornings begin with mist rolling through the valleys and the sound of river water. Days are filled with tending to our land, interacting with neighbors, and enjoying the simple pleasures of rural life.

Our guests often tell us that what they remember most isn't just the stunning scenery, but the warmth of village hospitality—sharing meals with our family, learning about local farming practices, and experiencing a way of life increasingly rare in modern India.

What Makes Sittong Special:

  • ✓ Off the beaten path—peaceful and uncrowded
  • ✓ Pristine natural beauty with rivers and forests
  • ✓ Close-knit community with strong cultural traditions
  • ✓ Access to tea gardens and trekking routes
  • ✓ Authentic village experiences unavailable in tourist areas
